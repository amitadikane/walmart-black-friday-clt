# Walmart Black Friday — Confidence Interval & CLT Analysis

**Domain:** Retail / Consumer Analytics  
**Tools:** Python, Pandas, NumPy, SciPy, Matplotlib, Seaborn  
**Focus:** Descriptive Statistics + Sampling Distributions + Confidence Intervals via Central Limit Theorem

---

## 📌 Problem Statement

Walmart wants to know whether spending habits differ between male and female customers on Black Friday, and whether Marital Status and Age also drive differences in purchase amount.

The population of interest is ~50 million male + 50 million female customers. We only have a large transactional sample and must use statistical inference (Confidence Intervals via CLT) to generalize.

---

## 🎯 Project Steps

1. Explore and clean the dataset
2. Profile purchase behavior across Gender, Marital Status, Age, City Category
3. Use the Central Limit Theorem to build sampling distributions of the mean
4. Construct confidence intervals (95% / 99%) for average spend by Gender, Marital Status, and Age group
5. Convert findings into actionable business recommendations

---

## 🛠️ Tech Stack

- Python, Pandas, NumPy
- SciPy
- Matplotlib & Seaborn

---

## 💡 Key Insights

- Clear difference in average purchase amount between male and female customers (quantified with CIs)
- Age groups and marital status also show distinct spending patterns
- Confidence intervals allow management to make decisions with measured uncertainty
